Examination Circular 2025 - Office of the Controller of Examinations

Attendance: With effect from the 2025 academic year, a student must have a minimum of 80% attendance in each course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

Condonation: Students with attendance between 70% and 80% may apply for condonation only with a medical certificate, on payment of a fee of Rs. 750 per course.

Revaluation: Applications for revaluation must be submitted within 5 days of the publication of results. The fee is Rs. 500 per paper.

Hall tickets: Hall tickets are available for download from the student portal three days before the first examination.
