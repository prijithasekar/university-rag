Examination Circular 2024 - Office of the Controller of Examinations

Attendance: A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

Condonation: Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with a valid reason, on payment of a fee of Rs. 500 per course.

Revaluation: Applications for revaluation must be submitted within 7 days of the publication of results. The fee is Rs. 400 per paper.

Hall tickets: Hall tickets are issued one week before the first examination and must be carried to every examination.
