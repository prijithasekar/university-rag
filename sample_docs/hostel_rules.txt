Hostel Rules and Regulations

Entry timings: Hostel gates close at 9:00 PM for first-year students and at 10:00 PM for senior students. Late entry requires prior written permission from the warden.

Visitors: Visitors are allowed only in the visitors' hall between 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM on weekends. Visitors are not permitted inside the rooms.

Mess: Mess fees are payable at the start of every semester. A student who is away for more than 7 continuous days may apply for a mess fee rebate through the warden.

Electrical appliances: Heaters, induction stoves and extension boards are not allowed in the rooms. Violations attract a fine of Rs. 1000.
